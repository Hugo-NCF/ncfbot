# Admissions Pathways

Scope: Public orientation to first-year, transfer, international, graduate, and admitted-student admissions. This resource summarizes pathways and current published deadlines without predicting individual decisions.
Verified through: 2026-08-31
Applies to: Current published admissions pages checked on 2026-08-31; deadlines and requirements are annual/cycle-sensitive and must be rechecked for the user's application cycle.

## Choose the correct admissions pathway

Before answering a deadline or requirement question, distinguish:

- first-year undergraduate;
- transfer undergraduate;
- international first-year or transfer;
- graduate applicant;
- admitted student completing next steps.

Do not assume one pathway's deadline or requirement applies to another.

## First-year undergraduate admissions

The first-year admissions page currently publishes deadlines for applications opening in 2026 for entry in 2027. For fall first-year admission, the page lists Early Action, Regular Decision, and a Final Deadline, with separate supporting-document and deposit dates. For spring first-year admission, it lists a final application deadline plus supporting-document and deposit deadlines.

Because these dates are cycle-specific, retrieve the current page before answering a deadline question and state the entry term/year explicitly.

## Transfer admissions

The transfer admissions page currently publishes separate fall and spring transfer deadlines and directs transfer applicants to transfer-specific application, scholarship, transfer-credit, and pathway information.

A transfer admission decision does not by itself establish how every prior course will apply to a degree. Do not guarantee transfer-credit applicability.

## International admissions

The main international admissions page publishes separate deadline tables for international applicants and distinguishes some applicants transferring an active SEVIS record from another U.S. institution.

International requirements involve immigration documentation and timing. Provide only published general information. Do not interpret immigration status, request passport/visa records, or decide an individual immigration case.

### Source discrepancy warning

At verification time, the main international-students page displayed a newer admissions-cycle table than at least one international freshman subpage that still displayed Fall 2026 dates. Do not silently merge these pages. For a current deadline question, prefer the current central international admissions page when it clearly applies, disclose the discrepancy if relevant, and direct the applicant to International Admissions for confirmation.

## Graduate admissions

The graduate admissions page publishes application requirements, program-specific prerequisites, English-language requirements where applicable, and admissions deadlines. Graduate requirements differ by program, so resolve the intended graduate program before giving program-specific requirements.

The graduate catalog is a stronger source for binding catalog requirements; the admissions page is appropriate for current operational application instructions and deadlines.

## Admitted students

The admitted-students page provides public next-step orientation such as enrollment-deposit, visit, scholarship/financial-aid, orientation, housing/dining, and new-student checklist links.

Record-specific status questions belong in the official application/status channel. Do not ask the user to provide an application ID or private application record.

## Admissions boundaries

Never:

- predict admission chances;
- guarantee admission, scholarship, housing, transfer credit, or visa outcomes;
- infer a decision from grades/test scores supplied in chat;
- claim a deadline from an old cycle is current;
- ask for private applicant credentials or records.
